## 🧬 About Me

Working on lab benches with samples and cell lines, I really enjoy the intellectual challenge of understanding biology while troubleshooting and optimizing my experiments. But at the end of the tunnel, I reflected that the high-throughput data produced to make sense of my work requires a bit of computational knack and knowledge. So, to claim full autonomy of my workflow, I've decided to prep myself for the shift of my exploration from pipettes to command lines.

This repository is intended to serve as my **virtual lab book and portfolio**, where I am documenting my journey of how, as a biologist, I'm moving forward to understanding the fundamentals of essential bioinformatics skills and how I'm extrapolating this learning to explore biological questions with publically available data.
